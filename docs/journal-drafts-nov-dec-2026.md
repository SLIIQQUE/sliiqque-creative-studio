- *What a launch campaign is:* A launch campaign is the set of assets built around a new product or brand: concept, art direction, a hero commercial, short-form content and stills that all follow the same rules, so the billboard, the Reel and the product page look like one company.

**What is not included**

- *Not in the price:* Media spend, logo design (SLIIQQUE does not design logos), extra videos at ₦40,000 each, extra revision rounds at ₦15,000, and rush delivery at +50%.

**Final Thoughts**

- *Compare like for like:* Ask any studio for a written scope that lists the commercial length, number of videos and stills, revisions, usage rights and delivery date. Then the price comparison means something. For a single commercial instead of a full launch, see AI commercials in Lagos.

## J2 · Thu 3 Dec 2026

- **Slug:** `five-questions-to-ask-before-hiring-an-ai-studio-in-lagos`
- **Category:** Process (links to AI Commercials)
- **Title:** Five questions to ask before hiring an AI studio in Lagos.
- **Card image:** `studio-ai-review-monitor.webp` · **Gallery:** `studio-storyboard-reference-portrait.webp`, `studio-graded-portrait.webp`

**Overview**

- *The short answer:* Before you pay a deposit to any AI creative studio in Lagos, ask five questions: can you hold the same face or product across six frames, what exactly does the price include, who owns the files, are your examples client work or labelled demos, and when is a traditional shoot the better choice.
- *Why these five:* They separate a studio with a repeatable method from one with a few lucky outputs, and they put the scope and the price in writing before any work starts.

**The five questions**

- *1. Can you show frame six, not frame one?:* The first image from an AI tool nearly always looks good. Consistency shows in the sixth. Ask to see the same face, product or garment across six frames, and how it is kept identical.
- *2. What does the price include?:* Running time, cutdowns, revision rounds, usage rights and delivery date should all be written down. SLIIQQUE's AI Commercial, for example, starts from ₦350,000 (US$650) and includes cutdowns for each platform, two revision rounds and commercial usage rights.
- *3. Who owns the files, and can I run them as ads?:* On one-off projects, deliverables should be assigned to you on full payment, including paid media use. Ask for it in writing.
- *4. Is this client work or demo work?:* Studios should label concept and demo work as exactly that. Be careful of examples with no named client and no label.
- *5. When would you tell me to book a studio instead?:* A good studio will say when exact fit, fabric or texture must be documented, or when a platform requires unretouched photography.

**Final Thoughts**

- *Put it in writing:* Ask for the answers to all five in writing. SLIIQQUE sends a written scope and price before any work starts. For costs, see how much an AI commercial costs in Lagos.

## J3 · Thu 17 Dec 2026

- **Slug:** `ai-product-photography-checklist-for-nigerian-ecommerce`
- **Category:** Photography (links to AI Photography)
- **Title:** AI product photography checklist for Nigerian e-commerce.
- **Card image:** `studio-process-flatlay-mosaic.webp` · **Gallery:** `ode-dropper.jpg`, `digital-human-fabric-detail.jpg`

**Overview**

- *The short answer:* Before you order AI product photography for an online store in Nigeria, have seven things ready: clear photos of every SKU, your logo and packaging files, a colour reference, the places the images will be used, any marketplace rules, the sizes you need, and a written scope. SLIIQQUE's Product Photo Pack covers 5 SKUs with 3 styled images each for ₦75,000 (US$140) in 5 working days.
- *Why it matters:* AI photography works from the photos and files you supply. The clearer the inputs, the more exactly the product is held identical in every scene.

**The checklist**

- *1 and 2, photos and files:* Clear photos of each product from several angles, plus your logo and packaging files exactly as they should appear. SLIIQQUE uses supplied logos and packaging as they are.
- *3, colour:* Name any colour that must not shift, with a reference, so it can be written into the product identity profile.
- *4 and 5, where the images live:* Store, marketplace, ads or social. Some marketplaces require unretouched photographs of the item, in which case a studio shoot is the right choice for those images.
- *6, sizes and crops:* List the sizes and aspect ratios you need so the files arrive ready to upload.
- *7, written scope:* Images per SKU, revision rounds, usage rights and delivery date, in writing.

**Final Thoughts**

- *Start small:* Many brands start with their best-selling five SKUs, then extend. See AI product photography in Lagos for scope, or compare it with a studio shoot in our cost and speed note.
